Paw Panic Support

Paw Panic is a paid iPhone puzzle game and the first title in the Nyan Nyan Nyan Series.

Contact

For support, bug reports, and App Store review contact, please email kurouchif@icloud.com.

How to Play

  • Tap a tile to flip that tile and its direct neighbors: up, down, left, and right.
  • Turn every ON tile off to clear the stage.
  • Clearing stages unlocks collectible cats in the World Cat Atlas.
  • Every 6 collected cats unlocks a breed note.
  • Bonus animals appear at collection milestones.

Features

  • 221 compact puzzle stages.
  • 222 collectible world cats.
  • Separate collection and guide screens.
  • Undo and hint tools.
  • English/Japanese language switch in Options.
  • No account required.

Troubleshooting

  • If a stage seems stuck, use Reset to restart the current stage.
  • If you want to start over, use Reset Progress after confirming the warning.
  • If sound or vibration feels distracting, adjust music, effects, and haptics in Options.
